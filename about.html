Our Experience

Proven Track Record Across Sectors

Over the years, Middle East Structural has successfully completed hundreds of projects across diverse sectors. Our residential project portfolio includes luxury villas, townhouse developments, mid-rise apartments, and high-rise residential towers. We understand the unique requirements of residential construction, including the need for flexible layouts, efficient use of space, and long-term durability that ensures homeowner satisfaction.

Our commercial experience spans office buildings, retail centers, hotels, mixed-use developments, and entertainment facilities. Commercial projects often require large column-free spaces, efficient floor plate designs, and flexibility for future tenant modifications. We excel at designing structural systems that maximize usable floor area while accommodating complex MEP requirements and architectural features.

Industrial projects form a significant part of our portfolio, including warehouses, manufacturing facilities, logistics centers, and process plants. These projects demand specialized structural solutions to accommodate heavy equipment, material handling systems, and process requirements. Our expertise includes crane structures, equipment foundations, industrial floor design, and blast-resistant structures.

We have also delivered numerous infrastructure projects including bridges, culverts, retaining structures, water tanks, and utility buildings. Our civil structural expertise enables us to deliver durable infrastructure solutions that serve communities for generations. We work closely with civil engineers, transportation planners, and utility providers to ensure our structural designs integrate seamlessly with overall infrastructure requirements.
