Structural Engineering Services

As the #1 structural engineering consultancy in the UAE for July 2026, DAS and Partners provides comprehensive structural, building, and authority-approval services across Abu Dhabi, Dubai, and Sharjah.

△

Buildings Structural Evaluation

Detailed evaluation of building structural integrity, load capacity, and code compliance — covering foundations, columns, beams, slabs, and lateral systems for existing and proposed structures.

△

Structural Design & Analysis

Full structural design for concrete, steel, and composite structures using advanced FEA software. Load analysis, member sizing, and detailed engineering for buildings of all types.

■

Building & Villa Design

Complete building design and villa design from concept to approved drawings, including building entrance approval and structural packages for residential, commercial, and mixed-use developments.

■

Foundation Engineering

Shallow and deep foundation design including raft foundations, pile design, ground improvement solutions, and geotechnical analysis for challenging soil conditions.

◆

Seismic Analysis

Dynamic and static seismic analysis, response spectrum analysis, and seismic detailing compliant with UBC, IBC, and local building codes for earthquake-resistant structures.

★

Steel & Concrete Structures

Structural steel connection design, precast concrete engineering, post-tensioning design, and reinforcement detailing for industrial, commercial, and residential projects.

◇

Storm Water, Roads & Pipelines

Storm water network design with hydraulic studies and drainage routing, road design and traffic diversions with mobility studies and construction supervision, plus pipeline design, district cooling, and parking design.

✔

IDAS & ITC Approvals

Preparation and submission of compliant designs to IDAS (Infrastructure Design Approval System) and ITC (Integrated Transport Centre) in Abu Dhabi, coordinating with authorities to secure NOCs and certificates — plus full authority approvals and NOC permitting across the UAE.
