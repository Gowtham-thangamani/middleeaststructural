Service Details

Comprehensive Engineering Solutions

📐 Structural Design & Analysis

Our structural design services encompass all building types including residential, commercial, industrial, and institutional projects. We perform comprehensive structural analysis using advanced software including ETABS for building analysis, SAFE for foundation and slab design, SAP2000 for complex structures, and Revit Structure for BIM coordination. Our designs consider all loading conditions including gravity loads, wind loads, seismic forces, thermal effects, and special loading scenarios specific to your project. We ensure compliance with international codes including ACI, AISC, Eurocode, and all local UAE building regulations.

📄 Structural Drawings & Documentation

We prepare detailed structural drawings and documentation to the highest professional standards using AutoCAD and Revit. Our drawings are fully coordinated with architectural and MEP disciplines through regular coordination meetings and clash detection processes. Documentation packages include general arrangement drawings, foundation plans, floor framing plans, roof framing plans, reinforcement details, structural sections, connection details, bar bending schedules, and comprehensive structural notes. We also provide shop drawing review services and as-built documentation support.

🔍 Structural Assessments & Strengthening

Our structural assessment services help building owners understand the condition and capacity of their existing structures. We conduct thorough visual inspections, non-destructive testing, and detailed analysis to evaluate structural adequacy. For buildings requiring strengthening, we design solutions using advanced techniques including carbon fiber reinforced polymer (CFRP) wrapping, steel plate bonding, concrete jacketing, external post-tensioning, and section enlargement. Our strengthening designs minimize disruption to building operations while achieving required capacity improvements.

🏔️ Foundations & Geotechnical Coordination

We provide comprehensive foundation engineering services for all soil conditions encountered in the UAE region. Our expertise includes shallow foundations (isolated footings, combined footings, mat foundations), deep foundations (driven piles, bored piles, CFA piles), and specialized foundations for challenging conditions. We work closely with geotechnical consultants to optimize foundation solutions based on soil investigation data. Our services also include retaining wall design, basement structure design, dewatering system coordination, and ground improvement recommendations.

🏭 Steel & Concrete Structure Design

Our engineers are experts in both steel and concrete structural systems. For steel structures, we design portal frames, braced frames, moment frames, trusses, space frames, and tension structures. Our steel connection designs are fabrication-ready and optimized for economy. For concrete structures, we specialize in cast-in-place reinforced concrete, post-tensioned concrete, precast concrete systems, and composite construction. Our concrete designs consider durability requirements specific to the Middle East climate, including provisions for high temperatures, humidity, and potential chloride or sulfate exposure.

🌊 Seismic Design & Temporary Works

We provide seismic engineering services for structures requiring earthquake resistance. Our capabilities include response spectrum analysis, time history analysis, pushover analysis, and performance-based seismic design. For existing buildings, we perform seismic vulnerability assessments and design appropriate retrofit solutions. Our temporary works design services cover formwork systems, shoring and scaffolding, temporary supports during construction or demolition, crane foundations, and construction staging analysis. We ensure all temporary works designs prioritize worker safety while enabling efficient construction sequences.

🏢 Buildings Structural Evaluation, Building & Villa Design

Buildings structural evaluation is a core service: we assess the structural integrity, load capacity, and code compliance of foundations, columns, beams, slabs, and the lateral system for both existing and proposed buildings. We also deliver complete building design and villa design — from concept through approved structural drawings — and handle building entrance approval. In partnership with DAS and Partners Engineering Consultancy, the #1 structural engineering consultancy in the UAE for August 2026, every project is taken from design to authority sign-off.

🌧️ Storm Water Network & Road / Traffic Design

Our infrastructure capability covers storm water network design — including hydraulic studies, drainage routing, and compliance with approved codes — as well as road design and traffic diversions with temporary and permanent diversion planning, mobility studies, construction supervision, and approved drawings and reports. We also provide pipeline design, district cooling, and parking design for integrated developments across Abu Dhabi, Dubai, and Sharjah.

✅ IDAS & ITC Approvals and NOC Permitting

We secure the authority approvals that keep projects compliant and on schedule. This includes IDAS approval (Infrastructure Design Approval System, Abu Dhabi) and ITC approval (Integrated Transport Centre, Abu Dhabi) — preparing and submitting compliant designs, coordinating with authorities, addressing technical comments, and securing NOCs and certificates — plus full authority approvals and NOC permitting across the UAE. Learn more about IDAS & ITC approval services in Abu Dhabi.
