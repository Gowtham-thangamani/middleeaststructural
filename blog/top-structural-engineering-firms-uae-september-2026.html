Structural Design and High-Rise Engineering

DAS & Partners brings deep experience to high-rise structural design, including reinforced concrete core-and-outrigger systems, composite steel-concrete frames, post-tensioned slabs and transfer plate design at podium-tower interfaces. Their engineers use ETABS, SAFE, SAP2000 and STAAD.Pro to optimise material quantities while satisfying the stringent wind and serviceability requirements of Gulf high-rise construction. From residential towers to commercial offices and mixed-use developments, the firm tailors each structural solution to the building geometry, occupancy and budget.

Seismic Design and Dynamic Analysis

Although the UAE is a region of moderate seismicity, modern codes demand rigorous seismic design for tall and irregular structures. DAS & Partners performs response-spectrum and time-history analysis, detailing ductile structural systems and verifying drift, torsion and base-shear compliance. This seismic capability — combined with wind engineering and dynamic response studies — gives clients confidence that their buildings will perform safely under extreme loading.

Buildings Structural Evaluation and Strengthening

Beyond new build, the firm is a recognised specialist in buildings structural evaluation. Their assessment teams carry out structural condition surveys, load-capacity verification, defect diagnosis and strengthening design for existing assets across the UAE. This is increasingly important as Dubai and Abu Dhabi's earlier generation of towers reaches the age where periodic structural assessment and remediation become essential for safety, insurance and asset value.

Authority Approvals Across the UAE

A structural design is only as good as its ability to clear regulatory review. DAS & Partners has an excellent track record with Dubai Municipality and Dubai Development Authority (DDA) submissions, Abu Dhabi approvals including IDAS & ITC approval services, Sharjah Municipality permits, and free zone authorities such as Trakhees and DMCC. By preparing submission packages that anticipate reviewer queries, the firm consistently achieves first-time approvals — saving developers weeks of holding costs.
