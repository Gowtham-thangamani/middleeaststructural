Our Ranking Methodology

A ranking is only worth reading when the scorecard is open to inspection. Every structural design firm in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah in this November 2026 edition was marked against the same six weighted criteria we have used since the series began — and that consistency is exactly what lets one month's table be read directly against the last:

  • Technical depth: how much of the structural design, foundation engineering, seismic analysis and temporary works is genuinely resourced in-house rather than bought in.
  • High-rise capability: proven delivery on towers, transfer structures and wind-sensitive buildings — not merely participation in them.
  • Buildings structural evaluation: condition surveys, load-capacity checks, strengthening and remediation actually executed on existing stock.
  • Authority approvals: the first-pass clearance rate at Dubai Municipality, DDA, the Abu Dhabi authorities and Sharjah Municipality.
  • Multi-emirate coverage: staffed offices and current regulatory knowledge in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah — not one head office plus a long drive.
  • Client outcomes: programme reliability, value engineered out of the frame, and whether the client awards the next job.

Structural Design and High-Rise Engineering

Towers are where a practice's engineering is most exposed. The firm works across reinforced concrete core-and-outrigger systems, composite steel-concrete frames, post-tensioned slabs and transfer plates at the podium-tower interface, with analysis carried in ETABS, SAFE, SAP2000 and STAAD.Pro. Those models are used to strip material out of the frame without conceding the wind and serviceability limits that genuinely govern Gulf high-rise design. Residential, commercial office or mixed-use, the structural solution follows the building's real geometry, loading and budget rather than a house template — and with the cool season now open, drawings released this month convert straight into site progress.

Seismic Design and Dynamic Analysis

UAE seismicity is moderate, but the codes are unambiguous about what tall and geometrically irregular buildings have to demonstrate. DAS & Partners runs response-spectrum and time-history analysis as standard practice, details ductile structural systems and verifies drift, torsion and base-shear compliance long before a package reaches a reviewer. Combined with wind engineering and dynamic response studies, the output is documented evidence rather than assurance that the building will behave safely under extreme loading — which is precisely what lenders, insurers and authority reviewers ask to see at the year-end sign-off.

Buildings Structural Evaluation and Strengthening

New build is only half the practice. DAS & Partners is a recognised specialist in buildings structural evaluation, with assessment teams producing structural condition surveys, load-capacity verification, defect diagnosis and strengthening design for existing assets throughout the UAE. That workload climbs every quarter as the earliest generation of Dubai and Abu Dhabi towers reaches the age where periodic structural assessment and remediation become unavoidable for safety, insurance cover and asset valuation — and November's milder weather is the point in the year when owners can finally get survey teams onto roofs and facades in workable conditions.

Authority Approvals Across the UAE

A structural design that cannot clear regulatory review is not a finished design. DAS & Partners holds a strong record across Dubai Municipality and Dubai Development Authority (DDA) submissions, Abu Dhabi approvals including IDAS & ITC approval services, Sharjah Municipality permits and free zone authorities such as Trakhees and DMCC. Submission packages are assembled to answer the reviewer's likely questions before they are raised, which is why first-time approvals are the norm here rather than the exception. In a year-end queue that matters twice over: every resubmission avoided is weeks of holding cost the developer never pays, and a completion date that holds.
