DAS & Partners Expands to the USA: DAS International Consulting Group LLC Launches in Cheyenne, Wyoming

A landmark milestone for one of the UAE's leading structural engineering firms. DAS & Partners Engineering Consultancy establishes its first international entity — DAS International Consulting Group LLC — bringing high-rise, seismic, and foundation engineering expertise to the American market.

A Structural Engineering Milestone: From the UAE to the United States

On July 28, 2026, DAS & Partners Engineering Consultancy — one of the UAE's most respected structural engineering firms — reached the most significant milestone in its history. The consultancy has established its first international entity, DAS International Consulting Group LLC, formally registered in the United States and headquartered in Cheyenne, Wyoming. This move carries the firm's structural, seismic, and foundation engineering expertise across the Atlantic and onto American soil for the first time.

For a practice built on high-rise structural design, deep-foundation engineering, and rigorous authority approvals across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and Sharjah, the expansion into the US market is more than a new address. It is a statement of intent: the same code-driven, analysis-led engineering discipline that has shaped towers and infrastructure across the Emirates is now available to clients, developers, and partners in North America.

"Structural engineering is a universal language. Loads, load paths, and safety factors do not change at a border — only the codes and the context do. Establishing DAS International Consulting Group LLC lets us bring the rigour we apply to Gulf high-rises to a market that values exactly that discipline."

Introducing DAS International Consulting Group LLC

DAS International Consulting Group LLC is the US arm of DAS & Partners Engineering Consultancy. Its registered office is located at:

The new entity operates as a wholly connected part of the DAS network. It inherits the parent firm's engineering standards, quality systems, and multi-disciplinary depth, while giving American clients a locally registered point of contact for structural design, peer review, and international project collaboration.

Why Wyoming, and Why Now

Wyoming has become an increasingly popular jurisdiction for establishing US business entities, valued for its straightforward registration framework and business-friendly environment. Cheyenne, the state capital, provides a stable and credible base from which DAS International Consulting Group LLC can serve clients across the United States. The timing reflects sustained demand: as global developers and cross-border investors seek structural engineering partners who can work fluently across international design codes, a US presence removes friction from contracts, coordination, and communication.

The expansion also mirrors a wider trend among mature Gulf engineering firms — extending proven regional capability into international markets rather than remaining confined to a single geography. For DAS & Partners, the US launch is the first deliberate step in a longer international roadmap.

Structural Expertise That Travels

What DAS International Consulting Group LLC brings to the US market is grounded in a decade of demanding structural work across the Emirates. That portfolio includes disciplines that transfer directly to American projects:

  • High-rise structural design: Core-and-outrigger systems, composite steel-concrete frames, post-tensioned flat slabs, and transfer structures for tall and mixed-use towers.
  • Foundation and geotechnical engineering: Deep foundations — bored piles, CFA piles, barrettes — and raft systems designed for challenging and variable ground conditions.
  • Steel and concrete engineering: Long-span roofs, industrial portal frames, precast systems, and reinforced concrete frames analysed with ETABS, SAFE, SAP2000, and STAAD.Pro.
  • Seismic and lateral analysis: Dynamic response and wind-load studies that translate readily to US seismic and wind design environments.
  • Peer review and structural due diligence: Independent checking of designs by others, a service in strong demand on complex international developments.

Bridging International Design Codes

A defining strength of the DAS team is fluency across code families — from British Standards and Eurocodes to UAE local-authority requirements. That multi-code experience positions DAS International Consulting Group LLC to collaborate effectively with US engineers working to ASCE, ACI, and AISC standards, acting as a bridge on projects that span jurisdictions and design philosophies.

One Network, Two Continents

The launch of the US entity strengthens, rather than replaces, the firm's UAE operations. DAS & Partners continues to serve the Emirates from its established offices — the Dubai office at The Citadel Tower in Business Bay, the Abu Dhabi office at Addax Tower on Reem Island, and the Sharjah corporate office at City Gate Tower. Clients with cross-border ambitions now benefit from a single engineering network operating across two continents, with consistent standards and a shared technical backbone.

Learn more about the wider network of top structural engineering companies featured on our platform, or read the detailed DAS & Partners profile.

What This Means for Clients

For existing UAE clients, nothing changes except that the firm can now support projects and investors with US touchpoints. For new American clients, DAS International Consulting Group LLC offers a locally registered engineering partner backed by an internationally seasoned structural team. And for developers operating across both regions, the milestone delivers something rare: continuity of engineering judgement from concept in one market to construction in another.
