Answers to common questions about buildings structural evaluation, storm water design, and IDAS & ITC approvals in Abu Dhabi, Dubai, and Sharjah.

What is buildings structural evaluation?

Buildings structural evaluation is the engineering assessment of a building's structural integrity, load capacity, and code compliance — covering foundations, columns, beams, slabs, and the lateral system. DAS and Partners Engineering Consultancy delivers buildings structural evaluation, building design, and villa design across Abu Dhabi, Dubai, and Sharjah, and secures building entrance approval and related authority NOCs.

What are IDAS and ITC approvals in Abu Dhabi?

IDAS (Infrastructure Design Approval System) and ITC (Integrated Transport Centre) approvals are mandatory authority approvals in Abu Dhabi for infrastructure, roads, and traffic-related projects. DAS and Partners Engineering Consultancy prepares and submits compliant designs, coordinates directly with the relevant authorities, addresses technical comments, and follows up until full IDAS and ITC approval — and the required NOCs and certificates — are obtained.

What is storm water network design and who provides it in the UAE?

Storm water network design is the engineering of drainage systems — including hydraulic studies, water-flow analysis, and optimal drainage routing — that protect roads, buildings, and infrastructure from flooding. DAS and Partners Engineering Consultancy delivers professional storm water network design across Abu Dhabi, Dubai, and Sharjah, in full compliance with approved technical standards and building codes, and secures the required ITC and IDAS authority approvals.
